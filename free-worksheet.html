Why this decision stalls everything

Couples start the conversation, can't agree, and quietly shelve the whole estate plan for another year. Meanwhile the default stays in place: if something happened to both of you tomorrow, a judge who has never met your family would decide who raises your children.

Here's the reframe that helps. You are not looking for the perfect parent. You're looking for a better outcome than a courtroom default. Almost any thoughtful choice clears that bar.

One thing worth knowing up front

A worksheet isn't a nomination. Neither is a trust — a trust cannot name guardians for minor children. Only a will can. Until your decision is written into one, the court is still the one deciding.

The worksheet gets you to a decision. Making it binding is the step after.
